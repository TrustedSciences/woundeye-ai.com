The burden

Why wound care can't wait

Chronic wounds develop silently and escalate fast. A small ulcer noticed late can become a $50,000+ episode — or an amputation. Early capture, honest measurement, and consistent tracking change that trajectory.

The silent progression
Diabetes → reduced sensation → unnoticed foot ulcer → infection → amputation. Each step is more expensive and harder to reverse than the last. The window to act is at the beginning, at home, when the wound is small.
The cost of waiting
A single manual wound assessment takes a clinician 20–30 minutes and costs $50–$75. One advanced ulcer episode exceeds $50,000, and a Stage 4 pressure ulcer costs $19,000 — much of it avoidable with early, consistent monitoring.
Prevention over reaction
WoundEye brings assessment to the wound instead of the wound to the clinic: photograph it at home, get immediate care guidance in seconds, share the record with your care team or our third-party care team before small problems become large ones.
