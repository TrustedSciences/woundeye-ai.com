The burden

Why wound care can't wait

Chronic wounds develop silently and escalate fast. A small foot ulcer noticed late can become an infection, a hospital stay — or an amputation. Each step is costlier and harder to reverse than the one before.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is WoundEye for emergencies?

No. Do not wait for a WoundEye answer if you or the person you care for has any of these signs — call 911 or go to the emergency room:

  • Fever, chills or shivering, or feeling very cold, with a wound
  • Confusion, unusual sleepiness, a fast heartbeat, fast breathing or shortness of breath
  • Clammy or sweaty skin, or extreme pain
  • Red streaks spreading out from the wound
  • Bleeding that does not stop with firm pressure

These can be signs of a serious infection or sepsis, which is a medical emergency.

When should I contact my doctor instead of waiting?

Contact your doctor or care team the same day if a wound shows signs of infection or is getting worse: redness, warmth or swelling that is spreading; pus or cloudy, yellow or green drainage; a bad smell; pain that is increasing; a wound that is getting bigger; or skin around it turning blue, black or very dark. You can still send a photo through WoundEye, but don’t let it delay that call.

I have diabetes. Is there anything I should do differently?

Yes. Diabetes can reduce feeling in the feet, so an injury may not hurt. Check your feet every day, including between your toes — use a mirror or ask someone to help. Contact your doctor promptly about any foot sore, blister, cut or crack, and don’t try to treat a foot ulcer at home. Have your feet examined by your doctor or a podiatrist at least once a year. WoundEye can help you photograph and track a foot wound, but it does not replace seeing your own care team.

Who actually looks at my wound?

A qualified clinician — a certified wound specialist on the AssureStage team, or your own care team if you choose to share with them. Every assessment is completed and signed off by a person, and any change is logged with the reason and the time.

How quickly will I hear back?

The app shows general care information as soon as you submit a photo. This is not a clinical assessment. A clinician’s structured assessment is returned to your phone the same day. If the wound changes or you become worried before then, contact your doctor — and for the emergency signs above, call 911.

Does WoundEye diagnose or treat wounds? Does it replace my doctor?

No. The WoundEye app is a wellness product. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease, and it is not a substitute for your own doctor or care team. Use WoundEye alongside your regular care, share the results with your doctor, and always follow the advice of the clinicians who are treating you in person.

Do I need special equipment?

No kit is required. WoundEye works on any iPhone, Android phone or web browser and guides you to take the photo from 15–30 cm away. Good, even lighting helps the clinician see the wound clearly.

Can I manage wounds for a family member?

Yes. The Caregiver plan gives access for several family members, with each person’s records kept safely separate. Please ask the person for their permission before photographing their wound and sharing it.

What happens to my photos and data?

Your records are yours. You can export them and ask for them to be deleted. WoundEye shows no advertising and never sells your data.
