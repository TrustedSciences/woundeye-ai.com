How it works

From your mobile to a clinician's answer

WoundEye brings the assessment to the wound instead of the wound to the clinic. No kit, no appointment — just the phone you already have.

  1. Capture

    Guided photo capture at 15–30 cm, with pain score and context in plain language. Carers can manage several family members, kept separate.

  2. Locate & log

    Pin the exact site on the body map. Each photo joins the wound's timeline with a patient-reported size — “~1.8 cm, about dime-sized.”

  3. Clinician review

    The photo goes securely to your own care team or ours. A qualified reviewer completes a structured assessment, on the record.

  4. Guidance & escalation

    A plain-language summary arrives the same day, with care recommendations and one-tap escalation to your doctor.

Five modules, one wound record

Designed to help patients, caregivers and clinicians capture, understand and track wound healing — together.

  1. 01

    Wound capture

    • Guided photo capture at 15–30 cm

    • Pain score and context in plain language

  2. 02

Integrations

Data interfaces & EHR readiness

Enterprise-grade foundations, ready to connect to clinical workflows — so a wound photo taken at a kitchen table can land in the chart.

  • FHIR R4

    Structured export in FHIR R4, JSON and CSV — EHR-ready by design.

  • Pull API & webhooks

    RESTful pull API plus HMAC-signed webhooks for event-driven integration.

  • Multi-tenant core

    PostgreSQL row-level security, JWT auth, role-based access. Enterprise-grade isolation.

  • Audit trail

    Every significant action logged — what happened, when, by whom.

  • One codebase, three modes

    React Native / Expo: consumer, employer and clinical modes on iOS, Android and web from a single build.

  • White-label ready

    Multi-tenant architecture supports partner branding and channel deployment.
