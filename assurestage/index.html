The clinicians behind every answer

AssureStage Clinical Wound Assessments

AssureStage is our team of certified wound specialists. When a WoundEye photo arrives, it is an AssureStage specialist — not software — who looks at the wound and decides what it is and what happens next.

The same team reviews bedside assessments from WoundRounds in hospitals and facilities, catching staging errors, missing measurements and gaps before they reach a survey, a claim or a courtroom.

  1. Reviews the photo and its site

    A certified wound specialist examines the photograph together with the exact location pinned on the body map.

  2. Determines type and stage

    The specialist decides what kind of wound it is and how advanced it is, and computes the PUSH healing score.

  3. Screens for infection

    The specialist screens the wound for signs of infection and flags any suspected infection in the assessment.

  4. Sets the follow-up

    The result goes back through WoundEye in plain language — what the wound is, what to do tonight, the signs that mean call 911, and the follow-up date.

Examples of what a wound specialist looks for. Clinical images are illustrative.

Precision routing

Worklists and clinician coordination

  • Check each photo in and confirm it is usable
  • File it to the right body site
  • Add it to the specialist’s worklist and track it until reviewed
  • Send reminders and schedule follow-ups

It simplifies, manages and scales the workflow — and makes no clinical decisions.
